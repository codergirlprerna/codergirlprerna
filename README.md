### 🚀 About Me

I'm an MCA graduate (2025) and freelance full-stack developer who likes building complete, production-shaped products — not just tutorials. My work spans live sports platforms, AI-powered agents, and a personalized digital gifting startup. When I'm not shipping code, I write about AI philosophy and the intersection of technology and art on Substack.

- 🏏 Built and ran **CricSense** live through IPL 2026 — real users, a 6-factor prediction engine, and a 26-table schema
- 🎁 Founder of **Sently** — a personalized digital gifting startup with 6 live templates
- 🤖 Building AI agents on n8n (Thaali — Indian food nutrition tracking with Gemini vision)
- ✍️ Also an imaginative poet — I write about code and art on Substack
